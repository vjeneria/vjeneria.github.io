// BS COMPUTER ENGINEERING THESIS · STI COLLEGE ORTIGAS-CAINTA · OCTOBER 2025

Arduino-Based Automated Abaca Stripping Machine with Drying System, Stripped Waste Remover and Storage

// Alinea · Del Monte · Eneria · Pe  |  Client: Brgy. Plaza Aldea, Tanay, Rizal

The Philippines supplies ~87.5% of the world's abaca fiber, yet processing remains largely manual — requiring farmers to hand-strip sheaths with serrated knives (10–20 kg/day), sun-dry fibers for 2–3 weeks, and manually clear blade waste. This machine, controlled by an Arduino Mega 2560, automates all three tasks to address physical fatigue, weather dependency, and production inefficiencies.

// What the Machine Does

  • Stripping — A Sharp IR Sensor detects inserted sheaths; rubber rollers and a gear motor automatically pull the sheath through a serrated knife to extract the fiber.
  • Drying — Stripped fibers fall into a chamber with an air fryer heating tube (regulated by a 110°C KSD301 thermostat) and two axial fans. A soil moisture sensor signals when drying is complete — reducing drying time from 2–3 weeks to just 10–30 minutes.
  • Waste Removal — An electric linear actuator with a brush sweeps residual waste off the blade after each sheath. Waste falls into a removable container for disposal or use as fertilizer.
