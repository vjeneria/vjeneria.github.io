THESIS PROJECT

// BS COMPUTER ENGINEERING THESIS · STI COLLEGE ORTIGAS-CAINTA · OCTOBER 2025

Arduino-Based Automated Abaca Stripping Machine with Drying System, Stripped Waste Remover and Storage

// Alinea · Del Monte · Eneria · Pe  |  Client: Brgy. Plaza Aldea, Tanay, Rizal

The Philippines supplies ~87.5% of the world's abaca fiber, yet processing remains largely manual — requiring farmers to hand-strip sheaths (10–20 kg/day), sun-dry fibers for 2–3 weeks, and manually clear blade waste. This Arduino Mega 2560–controlled machine automates all three tasks to address physical fatigue, weather dependency, and production inefficiencies.

// What the Machine Does

  • Stripping — A Sharp IR Sensor detects inserted sheaths; rubber rollers and a gear motor automatically pull the sheath through a serrated knife to extract the fiber.
  • Drying — Fibers fall into a chamber with an air fryer heating tube (regulated by a 110°C KSD301 thermostat) and two axial fans. A soil moisture sensor signals when drying is complete — reducing time from 2–3 weeks to just 10–30 minutes.
  • Waste Removal — An electric linear actuator with a brush sweeps residual waste off the blade after each sheath into a removable container.
